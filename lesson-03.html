LESSON 03 / 11

The Creator Mindset

创造者的心态

Whether you are taking this course to launch yourself into entrepreneurship, landing a better job, making high-level connections, or just to have fun…

You are a creator.

With that comes a lot of potential problems.

As a society, we are so used to being told what to do, when to do it, and how to do it.

Personal responsibility, self-management, self-reflection, and the ability to execute on your ideas are all necessities in this industry.

Personal growth = business growth = personal growth.

无论你上这门课是为了开启创业之路、找到一份更好的工作、建立高层次的人脉，还是单纯只是为了好玩……

你都是一个创造者。

随之而来的，是许多潜在的问题。

作为一个社会整体，我们已经太习惯被别人告知该做什么、什么时候做、以及怎么做。

个人责任感、自我管理、自我反思，以及把想法付诸执行的能力，是这个行业里必不可少的素质。

个人成长 = 事业成长 = 个人成长。

  • You are your own boss
  • You are your own project manager
  • You are your own content marketer
  • You are your own sales team
  • You are everything

Don’t know what to do? It’s your responsibility to create your own solution.

  • 你是自己的老板
  • 你是自己的项目经理
  • 你是自己的内容营销人员
  • 你是自己的销售团队
  • 你就是一切

不知道该怎么做？创造出属于你自己的解决方案，是你的责任。

Embrace The Unpredictability拥抱不可预测性

All of this is unpredictable.

You are going to feel lost, a lot.

Is there anything more that you could ask for?

You are blessed with the opportunity to chart unknown territory and make new discoveries.

Content creation and entrepreneurship are NOT taught in schools. Nor should they be.

This is very new territory. There are no formal college courses on it. Professors aren’t content creators. The landscape is changing every single day.

All we have is what online explorers have packaged up and put into courses. It’s all experience-based.

Yet, the only way you make life-changing discoveries is to go out there — get lost in the unknown, adopt the mindset of an explorer, and forge your own path.

这一切都是不可预测的。

你会经常感到迷失，而且是频繁地。

你还能要求什么呢？

你何其幸运，能拥有这样的机会，去开拓未知的领域，做出全新的发现。

内容创作和创业，学校并不教——而且本就不该教。

这是一片非常新的领域。没有正规的大学课程讲授它，教授们也不是内容创作者。这片图景每天都在变化。

我们所拥有的，只是那些在线探索者们把自己的经验打包、整理成的课程。这一切都是基于实践经验的。

然而，能带来改变人生的发现的唯一方法，就是走出去——迷失在未知之中，以探险家的心态，开辟出属于自己的道路。

The best thing you can do during these times is:

  • Brain dump journal about what you are struggling with
  • Turn what you write into content (telling yourself what to do)
  • Allow that process to help you discover the problem

Do not keep things in your head. Writing is what fills the gap in your thoughts.

By documenting your life, thought process, and experiences online — not only do you learn more about yourself, but you attract others that are experiencing something similar.

在这段时期，你能做的最好的事情就是：

  • 把你正在挣扎的问题，写成头脑倾倒式的日记
  • 把你写下的内容转化为内容作品（相当于告诉自己该怎么做）
  • 让这个过程帮助你发现问题所在

不要把事情都憋在脑子里。写作，正是用来填补你思维空白的东西。

通过在网上记录你的生活、思考过程和经历，你不仅能更多地了解自己，还能吸引那些正在经历相似事情的人。

There is only so much that I can teach you.

  • I can’t tell you the exact price you should put on your product
  • I can’t tell you the exact words to write that will bring in 1 million followers
  • I can’t tell you exactly what niche you should be in, or should be gravitating toward
  • I can’t tell you every single detail that should go into your landing page, emails, and funnels

I can only give you guidance, principle-based systems, and a starting point that we can iterate from.

我能教你的，终究是有限的。

  • 我无法告诉你产品该定价多少才准确
  • 我无法告诉你写下什么样的确切文字就能带来100万粉丝
  • 我无法确切告诉你，你的细分领域应该是什么、或者应该往哪个方向靠拢
  • 我无法告诉你落地页、邮件和转化漏斗里，每一个细节该怎么设计

我能给你的，只有方向上的指引、基于原则的系统，以及一个我们可以不断迭代的起点。

I’ve changed my bio — without exaggeration — at least 100 times.

I’ve built 10+ different products and services, and each one has done better than the one before it.

I’ve had content that gained 2 likes and lost me followers, but I’ve also had content that gained 438,968 likes and brought in 120,000+ followers.

This is only possible if you can stay consistent for over 6 months and remain open to learning from your failures.

Get used to it. Let go of the need to be perfect. Have faith in the process.

Adopt a 10-year mindset (you’ll hopefully be doing this for the rest of your life). You will never ever feel like you know exactly what you’re doing.

Just create.

毫不夸张地说，我至少改过100次个人简介。

我已经开发了10多种不同的产品和服务，每一个都比前一个做得更好。

我发过只获得2个赞、还掉粉的内容，也发过获得438,968个赞、带来12万多名新关注者的内容。

只有当你能保持超过6个月的持续输出，并愿意坦然接受、从失败中吸取教训时，这样的结果才有可能发生。

慢慢习惯这一点吧。放下对完美的执念，相信这个过程。

用10年的心态来看待这件事（希望你余生都会一直做下去）。你永远不会觉得自己百分百清楚在做什么。

只管去创造。

For the remainder of this course, I want you to take every tactic or method you hear outside of this content with a grain of salt.

Stop listening to people that tell you what your content, brand, or product is “supposed” to look like.

Put trust in my methods, let me teach you the principles, and use them to create your own way of doing things. That’s how you truly succeed at this game.

在这门课程剩下的部分里，我希望你对在别处听到的每一种战术或方法都保持一份审慎——姑且听之，不必全信。

不要再听信别人告诉你，你的内容、品牌或产品“应该”是什么样子。

相信我教你的方法和原则，并用它们创造出属于你自己的做事方式。这才是你在这场游戏里真正获胜的方式。

A Quote To Live By一句值得铭记的话

“As to methods there may be a million and then some, but principles are few. The man who grasps principles can successfully select his own methods. The man who tries methods, ignoring principles, is sure to have trouble.” — Ralph Waldo Emerson

“方法可能有成百上千种，但原则却屈指可数。掌握了原则的人，能够成功地选择属于自己的方法；而只尝试方法、却忽视原则的人，注定会遇到麻烦。”——拉尔夫·沃尔多·爱默生

Stop Thinking About Saturation And Finding Your Niche停止纠结饱和度，找到你的细分领域

Let’s do a little thought exercise.

How many people do you follow?

Probably between 100-500 people, more or less.

How many people do those people follow?

Probably the same.

How many people do creators themselves follow?

Again, the same.

让我们来做一个小小的思维练习。

你关注了多少人？

大概在100到500人之间，或多或少。

这些人自己又关注了多少人呢？

很可能也差不多。

那创作者本人又关注多少人呢？

同样，也差不多。

This begins to paint the picture of the creator economy — a self-sufficient utopia.

We all follow people based on our unique interests.

That alone should prove to you that saturation doesn’t exist for personal brands.

People have similar interests, but the combination of interests? Completely different.

这开始勾勒出创作者经济的图景——一个自给自足的乌托邦。

我们关注他人，都是基于自己独特的兴趣。

仅这一点，就足以向你证明：个人品牌根本不存在所谓的“饱和”。

人们的兴趣可能相似，但兴趣的组合方式呢？则完全不同。

If you can dig deep into your true curiosities, then it’s about turning them into money, and focusing on creating high-quality content, products, and services that you win with.

This is both the hardest and easiest part.

We have to win against ourselves. In our minds, there is no one else to compete with.

Throughout this course, we will lean on psychological principles to build a brand no one else can compete with.

This won’t happen overnight. Nor with one piece of content or one product — this is a lifelong game of iteration.

如果你能深入挖掘自己真正的好奇心，那么接下来要做的，就是把它转化为金钱——专注于打造能让你赢得胜利的高质量内容、产品和服务。

这既是最难的部分，也是最简单的部分。

我们真正要战胜的是自己。在我们的思维里，本不该存在其他竞争对手。

在这门课程中，我们将始终依靠心理学原理，打造一个无人能与之竞争的品牌。

这不会一蹴而就。也不会靠一篇内容或一款产品就实现——这是一场贯穿一生的迭代游戏。

When you find yourself feeling scattered, stressed, and anxious about your progress — zoom out.

Don’t view your content as something that gets posted and disappears.

Your content peaks 1, 2, 3, and 4 years down the line, which makes it irreplaceable.

Allowing longer form content to age on YouTube and podcasts is great for SEO, but that’s not the main benefit.

Once you’ve tested and validated the ideas you enjoy talking about, you can grow on other platforms with ease.

You already know which topics perform well.

Since I’ve spent a year growing on Twitter, I now have a database of viral posts that I can add spice to and re-publish on any platform.

This is only possible through the iteration of your content and ideas.

当你发现自己注意力涣散、压力倍增、为进展感到焦虑时——请把视角拉远，看向全局。

不要把你发布的内容，看作一发布就会消失的东西。

你的内容会在1年、2年、3年、4年后持续发挥影响力，这正是它不可替代的原因。

让长内容在YouTube和播客上慢慢“沉淀”，对SEO确实有好处，但这并不是它最主要的价值所在。

一旦你测试并验证了自己乐于谈论、并且行之有效的想法，你就能轻松地在其他平台上复制这种增长。

你其实早就知道哪些主题表现更好。

自从我花了一年时间在Twitter上积累，我现在已经拥有了一个“爆款内容数据库”，可以为其添点新意，然后在任何平台重新发布。

这一切，都只能通过对内容和想法不断迭代才能实现。

Prioritize Clarity优先追求清晰

Clarity, focus, and orderly consciousness are your best friend (in life and in business). Overthinking, entropy, and chaos are your biggest enemy.

Make a habit of pausing to write down all your thoughts and organize them. Make your mental processes physical.

清晰、专注和有条理的意识状态，是你最好的朋友（无论在生活还是事业中）。过度思考、混乱和无序，则是你最大的敌人。

养成暂停下来、把所有想法写下来并加以整理的习惯。让你的心理活动变得可视、可触。

The Opportunity Has Never Been Higher机会从未如此之大

I’m not saying this as some kind of marketing gimmick to get you to take action. I have full conviction that individuals can develop their skill set, land with even fewer followers than they used to need (it’s not much), and reverse the creator hierarchy as much as they’d like.

There has been a short-form content boom.

TikTok has creators growing faster than ever before.

Instagram is putting more effort into, and trying to copy, TikTok.

People can consume short-form tweets, TikToks, reels, and other content faster.

我这么说，绝不是某种鼓动你行动的营销噱头。我完全相信，每个人都能培养自己的技能，用比过去更少的粉丝量就能落地变现（真的不需要很多），并且只要愿意，就能颠覆传统的创作者等级体系。

短内容正迎来一波爆发式增长。

TikTok上创作者的成长速度，比以往任何时候都快。

Instagram正在加大力度打造Reels，试图复制TikTok的成功。

人们能够更快速地消费短推文、TikTok视频、Reels等内容。

Meaning they get exposed to more creators.

Meaning they follow and remember more creators.

Meaning there will be a lot of terrible content.

Meaning if you have a deeper understanding of content and offer creation, you will stand out and make 10x more than other creators.

这意味着他们能接触到更多创作者。

这意味着他们会关注并记住更多创作者。

这也意味着，会涌现出大量质量低劣的内容。

但这也意味着：如果你对内容和产品打造有更深的理解，你就能脱颖而出，赚到比其他创作者多10倍的收入。

As an example, Instagram recently rolled out a “reels bonus” to incentivize new creators (because demand for them was short-lived).

I tested it.

After 1 week I had 200K views on my reels.

That is what most creators will be happy with — relying on the platform to pay them.

Since I have my own content and product ecosystem, in that same week, I made $13,625 from free traffic on social media.

The new 1% won’t be one-hit-wonders.

They will be the ones that put Digital Economics into practice.

举个例子，Instagram最近推出了“Reels奖励金”，用来激励新创作者入场（因为平台对内容的需求本身就有周期性）。

我亲自测试了一下。

一周后，我的Reels获得了20万次浏览。

这正是大多数创作者会感到满足的结果——依赖平台来给自己发钱。

但因为我拥有自己的内容和产品生态系统，同样是这一周，我通过社交媒体上的免费流量赚到了13,625美元。

新一代的1%，不会是昙花一现的“一次性网红”。

他们会是那些真正把数字经济学付诸实践的人。

Join The 1%加入那1%

There would not be saturation if there were 50% creators and 50% consumers (creators are still consumers too).

But, only 1% are creators.

And if history repeats itself, that number will remain far below 50%.

The opportunity is that big. You will only realize it once you start.

Those that prioritize consistency will win.

如果创作者和消费者各占50%，那本不会出现饱和（因为创作者本身也是消费者）。

但事实上，只有1%的人是创作者。

如果历史重演，这个比例还会远低于50%。

机会就是这么大。只有当你真正开始行动，你才会意识到这一点。

那些重视持续输出的人，终将获胜。
