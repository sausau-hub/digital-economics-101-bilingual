LESSON 10 / 11

The Keys To Success

成功的关键

You make new discoveries by figuring things out for yourself.

No matter what information I give you throughout this course, you have to develop the crucial skill of “figuring it out.”

That is how you create the most value. If I can teach it to you, I can teach it to others.

Your job is to take everything I say here, understand it through direct experience, and create a way of doing things that is unique to you.

只有靠自己把事情弄明白，你才能真正获得新的发现。

不管我在这门课程里给你多少信息，你都必须培养出一项至关重要的技能——“自己把它琢磨透”。

这才是你创造最大价值的方式。如果我能把它教给你，我同样也能把它教给别人。

你的任务，是把我在这里说的一切，通过亲身实践去真正理解，然后创造出一套属于你自己、独一无二的做事方法。

Learning By Doing, Imitating, & Emulating通过实践、模仿与效仿来学习

“The greatest skill one can develop is decreasing the time between idea and execution.” — Dan Koe

Life becomes more meaningful when you can connect everyday thoughts, experiences, information, and stimuli to a project.

In other words, you are neurobiologically rewarded with dopamine and other chemicals when you apply what you learn.

A personal brand is a giant project that allows you to test, understand, and practice everything you learn.

Throughout this course, we will be focused on the principles and fundamentals of any given skill. Your job is to implement immediately by applying that information to your brand.

“一个人能培养出的最强大的技能，就是缩短从想法到执行之间的时间。” ——丹·科伊（Dan Koe）

当你能把日常的想法、经历、信息和各种外部刺激，与一个具体的项目联系起来时，生活就会变得更有意义。

换句话说，每当你把学到的东西付诸实践，大脑就会在神经生物层面奖励你多巴胺和其他化学物质。

个人品牌就是一个巨大的项目，能让你去测试、理解并实践你学到的一切。

在这门课程中，我们会聚焦于任何一项技能背后的原理和基本功。而你的任务，是把这些信息立刻应用到你的品牌上。

  • You learn web design by creating websites and landing pages.
  • You learn to write persuasively by practicing persuasive writing.
  • You learn storytelling by telling stories in your content and products.
  • 你通过动手做网站和落地页，来学会网页设计。
  • 你通过练习说服性写作，来学会有说服力地写作。
  • 你通过在自己的内容和产品中讲故事，来学会讲故事。

Awareness begets awareness.

You cannot become aware of what you need to learn unless you have applied and understood what you are currently aware of — otherwise it stays in your head and causes random problems that you are unconscious of.

意识催生意识。

除非你已经把当下所知道的东西付诸实践并真正理解，否则你根本无法意识到自己接下来还需要学什么——否则它就只会停留在你的脑子里，制造出一些你自己都没意识到的、莫名其妙的问题。

  • Research the basics, fundamentals, and principles online (Google, YouTube, books, book summary apps — like Shortform or Blinkist)
  • (If necessary) Research the best tool (software, product, etc) to get the job done at your experience level and watch a video on how the basics of that tool
  • Find 3-5 “mentors” to pull inspiration from (whether it be design, tone of voice, writing style, problems they use, benefits they use, anything)
  • Outline a real world project by combining everything you found by researching your mentors — this can be “fake” for practice or straight into your brand
  • Start the project, become aware of what step you are struggling with, and research how to do that specifically via Google or YouTube
  • Complete a first iteration of the project, publish it, see if it gets the results you’d like, and improve it as you learn more about the skill — this is a lifelong process.
  • 在网上研究基础知识、基本原理和方法论（Google、YouTube、书籍，或者 Shortform、Blinkist 这类书籍摘要应用）
  • （如有必要）研究最适合你当前经验水平的工具（软件、产品等），并看一段视频了解这个工具的基本用法
  • 找到3-5位可以从中汲取灵感的“导师”（无论是设计风格、语气、写作方式，还是他们所用的痛点、好处，任何方面都可以）
  • 把研究这些导师时收集到的一切整合起来，为一个现实项目列出大纲——这个项目可以是用来练手的“假”项目，也可以直接用在你自己的品牌上
  • 动手开始这个项目，留意自己卡在哪一步，然后针对性地通过 Google 或 YouTube 去研究怎么解决
  • 完成项目的第一个版本，把它发布出去，看看是否达到了你想要的效果，并随着你对这项技能的理解加深而不断改进——这是一个持续一生的过程。

An example of this process for creating a beautiful profile picture or header image would be:

以做一张好看的头像或主页封面图为例，这个流程可以是这样的：

  • Research graphic design or headshot photography principles or basics on YouTube — take notes on the levers you can pull with the least amount of effort to get the best results
  • Research a tool to create a profile picture or header image (PhotoShop or Canva) and watch a video on how to navigate it FOR a profile picture or header
  • Find 3-5 people on social media whose designs you like, specifically their profile picture or header image
  • Try to recreate the best parts of their image with your own
  • Watch videos or search for resources to help you navigate roadblocks you encounter (at a basic level, everyone has hit these roadblocks and you CAN find resources online, it may take a bit of digging.

This is the process for learning anything rapidly.

Visualize the end result, outline the project, and research to solve problems along the way to create your first iteration of a project.

  • 在 YouTube 上研究平面设计或形象照摄影的原理和基础知识——记下那些能以最小的努力换取最好效果的关键杠杆
  • 研究一个能用来做头像或封面图的工具（PhotoShop 或 Canva），看一段针对头像/封面图这一具体用途的操作教程
  • 在社交媒体上找到3-5个你喜欢其设计风格的人，特别关注他们的头像或封面图
  • 尝试用自己的照片，重现他们设计里最出彩的部分
  • 遇到卡壳的地方，就去看视频、找资源来帮自己解决（在基础层面上，几乎每个人都遇到过这些卡点，网上一定能找到相关资源，只是可能需要多挖一挖）

这就是快速学会任何一项技能的通用流程。

先在脑海中想象出最终成果，为项目列出大纲，然后在过程中不断研究、解决遇到的问题，做出这个项目的第一个版本。

Create Momentum创造势能

You can only create momentum and inspiration by doing.

Get in the habit of starting projects that you have no idea how to complete and figure out how to complete it.

Embrace the struggle. That’s where the most impactful lessons come from.

势能和灵感，只能靠“动手做”来创造。

养成这样的习惯：即使完全不知道一个项目该怎么完成，也先开始动手，然后在过程中一步步把它琢磨明白。

拥抱这个挣扎的过程吧。最有影响力的经验教训，恰恰都是从这里来的。

Documenting Your Life记录你的人生

When you applied what you’ve learned in a public setting, you are documenting the phases of your life.

By putting your creations out into the real world, your next creations will be built on (or iterated from) the previous creation.

Think of your idea generation, content curation and synthesis, life experiences, and creations that stem from those as documentation.

当你在公开场合把学到的东西付诸实践时，你其实是在记录自己人生的各个阶段。

把你的创作发布到真实世界里之后，你下一次的创作，都会建立在（或者说迭代自）前一次创作的基础之上。

把你的灵感产出、内容策划与整合、生活经历，以及由此诞生的创作，都看作是一种“记录”。

  • You are documenting your mind and consciousness when you make sense of ideas
  • You are documenting your failures, successes, and solutions to problems in your content
  • You are documenting systems for getting results in certain areas of life with your products
  • You are helping guide and order consciousness others throughout all of this — especially consulting
  • You are documenting and creating systems for yourself “behind the scenes” to produce better work and make more money

All of these things will hold more value than you could ever imagine in 2-3 years.

People remember the ideas that you introduced them to and how it changed their life (most won’t explicitly tell you this until years later).

People will get massive results from your product and praise you for your work.

Your products, content, brand, and life will improve with each and every iteration.

These are all incredible — and often unseen — benefits of this line of work.

  • 当你把想法理清楚时，你是在记录自己的思维和意识
  • 你的内容，记录着你的失败、成功，以及你解决问题的方式
  • 你的产品，记录着能在生活中某些领域产生成果的系统方法
  • 在这整个过程中，你也在帮助引导和梳理别人的意识——尤其是在做咨询时
  • 你也在为自己“幕后”记录和搭建一套系统，帮助自己产出更好的作品、赚到更多的钱

这一切，在2-3年后所展现出的价值，会远远超出你现在的想象。

人们会一直记得你带给他们的那些想法，以及它如何改变了他们的人生（大多数人往往要过好几年之后，才会明确地告诉你这件事）。

人们会从你的产品中获得巨大的成果，并因为你的作品而赞赏你。

你的产品、内容、品牌和人生，都会随着每一次迭代而不断进步。

这些都是这份工作里令人难以置信——却又常常被忽视——的收获。

It is You — but at scale.

Connected to the universe that the coders have created.

这就是“你”——只是放大到了更大的规模。

并且，你也因此与程序员们所创造的这整个互联网宇宙连接在了一起。

Write Everything Down把一切都写下来

Document and create. That’s it. That’s your life now.

Write all ideas, thoughts, and steps down as you come across them. We will have a place to document all of these things in the Notion Command Center that comes with the Bachelors curriculum.

记录，然后创造。就这么简单。这就是你现在的生活。

每当想法、念头和步骤出现时，就把它们写下来。学士课程会配套一个 Notion 指挥中心，作为专门记录这一切的地方。
