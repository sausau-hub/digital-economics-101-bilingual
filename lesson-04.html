LESSON 04 / 11

Skills, Expertise, & Curiosities

技能、专业知识与好奇心

The effectiveness of your brand, content, and offers are a combination of 3 things:

你的品牌、内容和产品，其效果如何，取决于三件事的结合：

  • Your skills
  • Your expertise
  • Your curiosities

Your skill level determines the quality of the value you deliver, your curiosities give you a unique perspective on certain topics, and your expertise allows you to monetize the attention you are grabbing.

In the first Digital Economics module we streamlined this to create your Niche Of One, discover your life’s work, and create impactful strategies that sell your products.

For now, I want to plant some seeds of awareness in your head.

  • 你的技能
  • 你的专业知识
  • 你的好奇心

你的技能水平决定了你所提供价值的质量；你的好奇心，赋予你对某些话题的独特视角；而你的专业知识，则让你能够把吸引来的注意力变现。

在第一个数字经济学模块中，我们已经把这一过程简化，帮助你找到属于自己的“独一无二细分定位”，发掘你一生的志业，并打造出能有效卖出你产品的内容策略。

现在，我想在你脑海里先埋下一些意识的种子。

As you go through this course, I would highly recommend starting to read up on the basics, principles, and fundamentals of certain skills.

You will not master these all in one go, but simply being aware of them will put you in the best position for growth.

Building a personal brand on social media gives you a place to practice ALL of these skills.

This is crucial.

Rather than endlessly learning and not knowing how to apply your skills — you can start applying what you learn immediately through your content, free products, and brand.

在学习这门课程的过程中，我强烈建议你开始阅读一些关于特定技能的原理和基础知识。

你不会一次性就掌握所有这些技能，但仅仅是对它们有所了解，就已经能让你处于最有利的成长位置。

在社交媒体上打造个人品牌，正好给了你一个练习所有这些技能的场所。

这一点至关重要。

与其无休止地学习却不知道如何运用你的技能，不如立刻开始——通过你的内容、免费产品和品牌，把学到的东西马上用起来。

Skills That Will Determine Your Success决定你能否成功的技能

Fortunately — building a full-fledged personal brand or creator business requires you to learn all of the skills that are necessary for success online.

Don’t let this overwhelm you. You don’t need all of it at the start. You can learn and implement these whenever you see fit.

The beauty in this: you have a meaningful project to work on, refine, and iterate for as long as you live.

Pursuing an intrinsic goal is a big key to happiness. It is how you reap the benefits of the good dopamine that comes from survival mechanisms in your brain.

Like working on a hobby project that you deeply care about, as opposed to a client project you grow tired of.

幸运的是，打造一个成熟完整的个人品牌或创作者事业，本身就要求你去学习在线取得成功所需的全部技能。

不要因此感到不堪重负。一开始你并不需要掌握全部——你可以在觉得合适的时候，随时学习和运用它们。

这件事美妙之处在于：你将拥有一个有意义的项目，可以用一辈子的时间去打磨、完善和迭代它。

追求内在目标，正是幸福感的关键所在。这就是你如何从大脑的生存机制中，获取那份美妙的多巴胺奖励。

就好比投入一个你真正热爱的兴趣项目，而不是一个让你渐渐厌倦的客户项目。

On Focus & Consciousness关于专注与意识

“The mark of a person who is in control of consciousness is the ability to focus attention at will, to be oblivious to distractions, to concentrate for as long as it takes to achieve a goal, and not longer. And the person who can do this usually enjoys the normal course of everyday life.” — Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi

“能够掌控自己意识的人，标志就在于：能够随心所欲地集中注意力，不受干扰，专注到完成目标所需要的时间为止，而不多花一分一秒。通常，能做到这一点的人，也更能享受日常生活本身。”——米哈里·契克森米哈赖

Here are the skills that will boost the impact and income from your online endeavors:

  • Copywriting (persuasive writing)
  • Email marketing (for trust-building and sales)
  • Sales (for closing deals and networking properly)
  • Web design (for landing pages and a personal website)
  • Social media growth (for sending traffic and audience building)
  • Graphic design (for visuals and brand assets)
  • Marketing & advertising (for getting people to act)

以下是能提升你线上事业影响力和收入的技能：

  • 文案写作（有说服力的写作）
  • 邮件营销（用于建立信任和促成销售）
  • 销售（用于顺利成交和建立人脉）
  • 网页设计（用于落地页和个人网站）
  • 社交媒体增长（用于引流和积累受众）
  • 平面设计（用于视觉呈现和品牌资产）
  • 营销与广告（用于促使人们采取行动）

Throughout Digital Economics, we will be learning all of these in a streamlined — low stress — manner.

You learn these skills best by learning the principles, starting a real world project (a personal brand), and refining your skills with time and experience.

These can all be freelanced or consulted once you get the hang of them, or once you outgrow needing them yourself.

The more you practice these and get results for yourself — the more you understand the nuance of how these skills interact with a specific business.

That is how you develop your own unique system and increase your prices if you want to freelance or consult with these down the road.

在整个《数字经济学》课程中，我们会以一种精简、低压力的方式，逐一学习这些技能。

学习这些技能的最佳方式是：先理解原理，然后启动一个真实世界的项目（也就是你的个人品牌），再随着时间和经验不断打磨你的技能。

一旦你掌握了这些技能的门道，或者你自己已经不再需要它们，这些技能全都可以拿去做自由职业或咨询服务。

你练习得越多、越能为自己带来实际成果，你就越能理解这些技能在具体业务中相互作用的细微之处。

这正是你打造出属于自己独特系统、并在未来想做自由职业或咨询时提高报价的方法。

All of these skills rely on a set of meta-skills:

  • Grabbing and holding attention
  • Storytelling and persuasion
  • Psychology & human behavior
  • Self-awareness and observation
  • Philosophy and spirituality

所有这些技能，最终都依赖于一组元技能：

  • 抓住并保持他人的注意力
  • 讲故事与说服力
  • 心理学与人类行为
  • 自我意识与观察力
  • 哲学与精神层面的思考

In other words, you need to understand how humans make sense of and interpret what they see.

You need to understand different perspectives and viewpoints so you can navigate topics with expertise.

You need to structure your message (throughout your entire brand, content, and funnel) in a way that leads readers toward a beneficial desired outcome.

None of this will work if you create what you think will help others VS what is actually going to help others.

换句话说，你需要理解人类是如何理解和解读他们所看到的东西的。

你需要了解不同的视角和观点，这样你才能凭借专业知识，游刃有余地驾驭各种话题。

你需要在整个品牌、内容和转化漏斗中，用一种能引导读者走向有益预期结果的方式，构建你的信息表达。

如果你创造出来的，只是你自认为能帮到别人的东西，而不是真正能帮到别人的东西，那么这一切都不会奏效。

Specific Knowledge具体知识

You don’t have to do all of these things yourself. You can outsource them.

But, the process of “figuring it out” is how you acquire specific knowledge. That is how you make new discoveries and help others navigate the path effectively.

你不必事事亲力亲为，这些都可以外包出去。

但“亲自摸索出方法”的过程，正是你获取“具体知识”的方式。正是通过这个过程，你才能有新的发现，并且能有效地帮助他人走好这条路。

How Your Curiosities Play A Role你的好奇心将发挥怎样的作用

One big problem I see all the time is that people don’t know how to talk about what they love (while still maintaining authority).

It’s pretty simple, you just have to zoom out.

Stop thinking of your content or brand as a static thing from day to day.

It grows and evolves over time. Your content doesn’t just disappear after you write it. You refine your ideas, post them again, and build a library that displays your authority, interests, and personality.

Some content will stick in people’s heads and do well — some content won’t. That’s how it goes.

我经常看到的一个大问题是：人们不知道该如何谈论自己所热爱的事物（同时又保持权威感）。

其实很简单，你只需要把视角拉远、看向全局。

不要把你的内容或品牌，看作是一天天一成不变的静态存在。

它会随着时间不断成长和演变。你的内容并不会在你写下来之后就消失——你可以不断完善自己的想法，重新发布，逐渐积累起一个能展现你的权威性、兴趣和个性的内容库。

有些内容会留在人们的脑海里、表现出色，有些则不会。这就是常态。

You incorporate your curiosities by being observant and using them to bring a new perspective to a problem that people face.

Your curiosities intersect with your interests, skills, and expertise — if and only if you are trying to find a way to connect them.

Here is a general way to incorporate your curiosities into your brand:

你可以通过观察力，把自己的好奇心融入进去，为人们正在面对的问题带来一个全新的视角。

只有当你努力去寻找连接的方式时，你的好奇心才会与你的兴趣、技能和专业知识真正产生交集。

下面是一种把好奇心融入你的品牌的通用方法：

  • Write out 2-3 of your interests (the things you LOVE talking about)
  • Write out 2-3 options for eventual monetization (skills or interests like health or performance coaching)
  • Think big and small — write out broader interests and skills, then write out narrower interests and skills related to the 2-3 points you already wrote out. This starts to create a web of content ideas and curiosities to pursue
  • Keep an eye out for books, content, podcasts, and life experiences that check one of those boxes
  • Make a note of it and create some form of content around it

This is a watered-down version of the system we will be implementing in the Bachelors tier to make this process seamless.

  • 写下你感兴趣的2-3件事——那些你喜欢谈论的话题
  • 写下2-3个未来可能变现的技能或兴趣选项，比如健康或绩效教练
  • 由大到小地思考——先写下更广泛的兴趣和技能，再写出与你刚才那2-3点相关、范围更窄的兴趣和技能。这样就开始织就一张由内容创意和好奇心追求交织而成的网络
  • 留意书籍、内容、播客和生活经历中，能与上述任意一点对上号的内容
  • 把它记下来，并围绕它创作出某种形式的内容

这只是一个简化版本，在学士课程阶段，我们会实施一套更完整的系统，让这个过程变得更加无缝顺畅。

You Are Building A Personal Brand你正在打造个人品牌

Niche brands have their place, but this is for people that want to harness the internet to do what they love (and pursue life’s work).

It is difficult to put YOU in a box — if not impossible. You are ALLOWED to try different things and pivot whenever you want. You are a human that evolves with time.

Why would that be any different in a digital brand?

Through practice and breaking limiting beliefs, this will become a reality.

细分品牌自有其价值，但这门课是为那些想借助互联网做自己喜欢的事情（并追求自己一生的志业）的人准备的。

想把“你”这个人限定在一个固定的盒子里，即便不是不可能，也是非常困难的。你完全被允许去尝试不同的事物，并在任何时候转向——你是一个会随时间不断进化的人。

数字品牌又为什么要有所不同呢？

通过不断实践、打破限制性信念，这一切终将成为现实。

Solving Your Own Problems And Selling The Solution解决自己的问题，并把解决方案卖出去

If you don’t have a clear offer to sell — or a skill that you are hell bent on making money with — there is another route you can take.

This route is an all-in-one “one-person” business model approach.

You are leveraging your own ability to solve your own problems, documenting the process via content, and creating a product that you can sell to your past self.

This is:

如果你还没有一个明确、可以出售的产品或服务，也还没有一项你下定决心要靠它赚钱的技能，你还有另一条路可以走。

这条路是一种“一体化”的“一人商业模式”。

你利用自己的能力来解决自己的问题，通过内容把这个过程记录下来，并最终打造出一款能够卖给“过去的自己”的产品。

这包括：

  • Personal growth
  • Social media growth
  • Authority building
  • Market research
  • Marketing
  • Creating offers

All in one. Good.

If you attract people with similar interests to you, educate them, and give them a process that cuts their learning time by 2-3 years, they will pay you for it.

  • 个人成长
  • 社交媒体增长
  • 建立权威
  • 市场调研
  • 营销
  • 打造产品

以上全部，一步到位。很好。

如果你能吸引到和你兴趣相投的人，教育他们，并给他们一套能把学习时间缩短2-3年的方法，他们就会心甘情愿为此付费。

For example: I’ve been passionate about health since I was an adult.

I have 10 years of experience.

I’ve tried all the diets, training programs, and everything else. I have a very unique perspective to share with results to show for it.

Even though I talk about business and self-improvement, I could still sell six-figures worth of health product a month or two from now.

(Of course, this is considering I have an audience and vast marketing knowledge)

My point is — my audience likes me. Everyone has burning biological problems in the eternal markets. If I can position myself correctly, I can create a product on anything I want in these markets.

My personal brand is what sets me apart from others. People will buy my health products and not someone else’s — otherwise identical — simply because I made them aware of their own problems and educated them on how to overcome it through my content.

举个例子：从成年以后，我就一直对健康充满热情。

我在这方面已经积累了10年的经验。

我几乎尝试过所有的饮食法、训练计划以及其他一切相关的东西。我有一个非常独特的视角，并且能用实实在在的结果去证明它。

即便我平时谈论的是商业和自我提升，我依然能在一两个月内，卖出价值6位数的健康类产品。

（当然，这是建立在我已经拥有观众基础、并具备丰富营销知识的前提之上）

我想说的是：我的观众喜欢我。每个人都有亟待解决的、根植于生物本能的问题——这是一个永恒的市场。只要我能正确定位自己，我几乎可以在这些永恒市场中，围绕任何我想做的主题打造产品。

我的个人品牌，是我区别于其他人的地方。人们会买我的健康产品，而不是别人那些本质上大同小异的产品——原因很简单：是我让他们意识到了自己的问题，并通过我的内容教会了他们如何去克服它。

The Best Products最好的产品

The best products solve a problem that impacts a specific person’s direct experience in a positive way.

By solving your own problems, you do this automatically (and in a unique way).

This makes it seamless to target a problem in the eternal markets and position it towards a beginner market. Nearly guaranteed sales if you understand the rest of Digital Economics 101 on an experiential level.

最好的产品，能以一种积极的方式，解决直接影响到某个具体的人、真实存在于他们生活体验中的问题。

通过解决你自己的问题，你会自然而然地、以一种独特的方式做到这一点。

这让你能毫不费力地在永恒市场中精准锁定问题，并面向入门级受众进行定位。只要你从体验层面真正理解了《数字经济学101》剩下的内容，几乎就能确保后续的销售。
