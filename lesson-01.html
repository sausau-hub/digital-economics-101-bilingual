What To Expect In Digital Economics

本课程有哪些内容

Digital Economics is the result of 5+ years of iteration in freelancing, consulting, digital product sales, multiple failed brands, community building and audience growth into simple systems that have proven to get results time and time again.

I have used these exact systems and strategies to generate 6 figures in each of these domains (freelancing, consulting, digital product sales, community memberships, and brand deals).

I also used these systems to pull in my first $50K month while “working” less than 2 hours a day.

Nothing I have done is special or anywhere near off limits for others to achieve.

However, I have struggled with some problems.

My mind was filled with questions and lack of clarity during many low moments in business.

I know how it is — and I still struggle to this day — but I know the answers that most people are looking for when it comes to doing what they love and making more than enough money to live a fulfilling life.

My goal is to give you all the resources you need (that you can come back to at any time) when you feel lost, want to make more sales, want to grow faster, or want to harness your creative energy for spreading a good word in this world.

数字经济学是我在自由职业、咨询、数字产品销售等领域历经5年多迭代的成果。

产品销售、多个失败的品牌、社区建设和受众增长的经验，被浓缩转化为简单的系统，这些系统已被证明能一次又一次地取得成果。

我正是运用这些系统和策略，在每个领域（自由职业、咨询、数字产品销售、社区会员和品牌合作）都实现了6位数收入。

我还用这些系统，在每天"工作"不到2小时的情况下，实现了自己的第一个5万美元月收入。

我所做的一切都不是特别的，也不是别人无法实现的。

然而，我也曾遇到过一些问题。在商业上的许多低谷时刻，我的脑海里充满了疑问，缺乏清晰的方向。

我知道这种感受——直到今天，我仍在努力——但当涉及到做自己喜欢的事情、赚足够多的钱过上充实的生活时，我知道大多数人正在寻找的答案。

我的目标是给你所有你需要的资源，让你随时都能回来查阅：当你感到迷茫、想做更多销售、想更快地增长，或者想利用你的创造力在这个世界上传播好的价值时。

Who Will Benefit The Most From This Course?谁将从这门课程中受益最大？

While I believe that everyone should learn how to navigate the digital economy, this course is for a select few people.

I like to call them value creators.

In other words, people that are trying to improve the lives of others by consuming, synthesizing, developing, creating, and selling value.

虽然我认为每个人都应该学习如何驾驭数字经济，但这门课程是为少数人准备的。

我喜欢称他们为价值创造者。

换句话说，就是那些试图通过消费、综合、开发、创造和销售价值来改善他人生活的人。

Who is this for exactly? People who want to:这到底是为谁准备的？为那些想要的人：

  • Develop their personal and professional skill set
  • Learn how to ethically persuade, influence, and sell
  • Make a positive contribution to someone’s life or business
  • Raise their level of consciousness and knowledge surrounding topics they are passionate about
  • Gain clarity on their purpose and life’s work while harnessing the power of the internet to pull in enough money to sustain that work
  • Build a public resume through value creation and modern opportunities find you
  • Learn how to create impactful content by understanding human nature and psychology
  • Build a one-person business as a freelancer, consultant, online educator, or knowledge worker
  • 培养他们的个人和专业技能
  • 学习如何在道德的前提下说服、影响和销售
  • 为某人的生活或事业做出积极贡献
  • 提高他们对自己热爱主题的意识和知识水平
  • 在利用互联网力量的同时，明确自己的目标和人生工作，并筹集足够的资金来维持那项工作
  • 通过价值创造建立公开简历，让现代机会找到你
  • 学习如何通过了解人性和心理来创造有影响力的内容
  • 以自由职业者、顾问、在线教育者或知识工作者的身份建立个人企业

Who is this not for?这不是为谁准备的？

  • Those that don’t see the value in personal growth and positive impact
  • Those that intentionally — or unintentionally — seek to harm others through negative content (certain memes, pornography, etc)
  • Those that have a distorted perception of money, sales, and taking impact of what you put out into the world seriously
  • On the business side of things — those that are set on pursuing a hyper-focused niche for freelancing, agency work, and other things

On that last point — this is for those that want to talk about their interests and curiosities while selling life-changing products (not those that are focused on only talking about the niche topic related to what they are selling — there is nothing wrong with this, just clarifying who will see the best results from this).

  • 那些没有看到个人成长和积极影响价值的人
  • 那些有意或无意地试图通过负面内容（某些梗图、色情内容等）伤害他人的人
  • 那些对金钱、销售，以及认真对待自己作品所带来影响力这件事，存在扭曲认知的人
  • 在商业方面，那些执意追求自由职业、代理工作等高度聚焦细分领域的人

关于最后一点——这是为那些想在销售能改变生活的产品的同时，也谈论自己的兴趣和好奇心的人准备的（而不是那些只专注于谈论与他们所销售产品相关的细分话题的人——这样做本身没有错，只是想澄清谁会从中看到最好的效果）。

What Will You Learn?你会学到什么？

There are a lot of moving parts to the digital economy. This course reflects that and is structured accordingly.

数字经济涉及许多不断变化的部分，这门课程也反映了这一点，并据此进行了结构设计。

In Digital Economics 101 you will learn:在《数字经济学101》中，你将学到：

  • The foundational information for succeeding as a value creator
  • The unconventional mindset that you will need to adapt to succeed in this uncertain and unpredictable line of work
  • The relationship between your skills, interests, and expertise (and how they create an irreplaceable brand)
  • A breakdown of the skills, terms, and methods for communicating your value and putting a price tag on it
  • The true (and only) way to make a sustainable income source with your time and creations
  • The key to becoming someone who is taken seriously for their “original” and impactful thoughts, ideas, and work
  • Actionable steps that you can take right now to start growing on social media, networking with high-level individuals, and making your first sale
  • Digital Economics is a free mini-course that is available to everyone. It acts as a primer for those that plan to take the Bachelors and/or Masters degree curriculum.
  • 作为价值创造者取得成功所需的基础信息
  • 在这种不确定且不可预测的工作路线中，你需要采取的非传统心态
  • 你的技能、兴趣和专业知识之间的关系（以及它们如何共同打造一个不可替代的品牌）
  • 关于传达你的价值、并为其明码标价所需的技能、术语和方法的细分讲解
  • 用你的时间和创作打造可持续收入来源的唯一真正方法
  • 成为一个因原创且有影响力的思想、想法和作品而被认真对待的人的关键
  • 你现在就能采取的行动步骤，开始在社交媒体上成长、与高层人士建立联系，并完成你的第一笔销售
  • 《数字经济学》是一门面向所有人免费开放的迷你课程，它起到入门作用，适合那些计划攻读学士和/或硕士课程的人。

In the Bachelors Curriculum you will learn:在学士课程中，你将学到：

  • How to create an irreplaceable personal brand based on the combination of your interests, skills, and expertise
  • How to attract an audience — or community — of like-minded people that have similar goals, interests, and a vision for their future
  • How to generate unlimited content ideas, articulate your thoughts, and package them up into digital assets that grow in value over time
  • My process for creating a content ecosystem that automates trust building, promotions, and sales without gimmicks, tricks, or hacks
  • The best way to create long, medium, and short-form content that can be distributed on all social platforms (without spending all day writing, filming, editing, or designing)
  • How to pivot your brand, content, and products as you grow as an individual — your brand will evolve with you (as will your income)

This is all real-world project-based for rapid learning and understanding.

This is fast-paced and carefully crafted to help you see results faster than you would studying by traditional methods — albeit you will go through much uncertainty at times (this is necessary, embrace it).

We will be implementing my time-tested systems into your brand and refining them to your unique needs. This is fleshed out in the Notion Command Center that you will (hopefully) be using and refining for a long time.

By the end of the Bachelors curriculum, you will have clarity on the next 3 years of your brand, a growing and (monetizable) audience, and a content creation system that will separate you from the sea of other creators.

  • 如何基于你的兴趣、技能和专业知识的组合，打造一个不可替代的个人品牌
  • 如何吸引目标、兴趣和未来愿景相似的志同道合的观众——或者说社区
  • 如何产生无限的内容创意，清晰表达你的想法，并将其打包成能随时间不断增值的数字资产
  • 我打造内容生态系统的方法，该生态系统能在没有噱头、伎俩或投机取巧的情况下，自动建立信任、进行推广和销售
  • 创作可在所有社交平台分发的长、中、短篇内容的最佳方式（无需整天写作、拍摄、剪辑或设计）
  • 随着你个人的成长，如何调整你的品牌、内容和产品——你的品牌会与你一同进化（你的收入也是）

这一切都基于真实世界的实际项目，帮助你快速学习和理解。

这门课程节奏快、设计精心，能帮助你比传统学习方式更快看到结果——尽管你有时会经历很多不确定性（这是必要的，欣然接受它吧）。

我们会把经过时间检验的系统运用到你的品牌上，并根据你的独特需求进行打磨。这些内容都会在 Notion 指挥中心里详细呈现，希望你能长期使用和精炼它。

到学士课程结束时，你将对自己品牌未来3年的方向有清晰的认识，拥有一个不断增长且可变现的受众群体，以及一套能让你从众多创作者中脱颖而出的内容创作系统。

In the Masters Curriculum you will learn:在硕士课程中，你将学到：

  • How to package up your knowledge into a consulting, freelance, or digital product that outperforms other products on the market
  • How to build your own sales page, email funnel, and promotion system that fits your content strategy to perfection
  • A streamlined way of leveraging ALL of your distribution network for a potential 6 figure launch (and how to extend the “shelf life” of your products and services to keep pulling in revenue)
  • How to turn your readers into product buyers with high ticket service clients — rounding out your sales system and increasing the lifetime value of each customer
  • The best way to promote your products and services without adding more to your daily work (and without sounding salesy, needy, or spammy)

The Masters curriculum is best suited for those that have offers that they can’t charge $5K+ for, aren’t making the sales they want, or aren’t confident in their skill set to the point where they are ready to monetize (and skip the beginner mistakes).

  • 如何将你的知识打包成咨询、自由职业或数字产品，使其表现优于市场上的其他产品
  • 如何构建完美契合你内容策略的销售页面、电子邮件转化漏斗和推广系统
  • 一种简化的方法，用来调动你所有的分销网络，实现潜在的6位数发布（以及如何延长产品和服务的"保质期"以持续带来收入）
  • 如何将你的读者转化为产品买家，进而成为高价服务客户——完善你的销售体系，提升每位客户的终身价值
  • 在不增加日常工作量的情况下推广你的产品和服务的最佳方式（并且听起来不会像在推销、乞求或发垃圾信息）

硕士课程最适合那些手上有产品/服务却无法卖到5000美元以上、没有实现自己想要的销售额，或者对自己的技能还不够自信、尚未准备好变现（并想跳过初学者常犯错误）的人。
